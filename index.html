Questions

The things people ask us most

How much will my repair cost?

The diagnostic is free. Once we know the fault we give you one fixed price, and we do not start until you agree to it. There are no surprise charges at the end.

How long does it take?

Most screen, battery and software jobs are done the same day when we have the part in stock. If a part has to be ordered it is usually two to three days, and we tell you before you leave the machine.

Will I lose my files?

No. Every repair starts with a conversation about your files, and we never erase a drive without telling you first. If the drive itself has failed, we can usually still recover what is on it.

Do you repair MacBook and Apple?

Yes. Screens, batteries, keyboards, charging faults and data recovery on MacBook and iMac, alongside every Windows brand.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes, across Jbeil, Keserwan and Batroun, for installations, networks, cameras and anything that cannot travel. Companies can take a monthly contract with priority visits.

How can I pay?

Cash, card at the shop, OMT, Whish Money or bank transfer. Online orders are paid on delivery or on pickup, never on the website. Invoices available for companies.

Which languages do you speak?

Arabic, French and English, at the counter and on WhatsApp.
