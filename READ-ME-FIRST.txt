CST COMPUTER — WEBSITE PROJECT
================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
----------------------
index.html      Your website. This is the only file your customers see.
editor.html     Your PRIVATE price editor. Do NOT upload this one.
images/         Empty — put your photos here before uploading. See below.


BEFORE YOU PUT THE SITE ONLINE
--------------------------------
1. Open index.html in Notepad or VS Code (not Word — it breaks quotes).
2. Search for CHANGE-ME. Fill in the 3 things it finds:
     - your email
     - your opening time (closing 19:30 is already set)
     - your Facebook / Instagram links
3. Delete the yellow "Setup" box near the top of the page
   (marked "DELETE THIS WHOLE BLOCK BEFORE YOU GO LIVE").
4. Add your photos to the images folder:
     images/hero.jpg         your CST brand card (shown as-is, not cropped)
     images/logo.png         your logo, transparent background
     images/storefront.jpg   the shop from the street
     images/bench.jpg, accessories.jpg, laptops.jpg, team.jpg  (optional)
   Each photo appears on the site by itself — no code needed.
5. If you EVER replace a photo later, open index.html, find:
     var ASSETS_V = "1";
   and change it to "2", "3", etc. Otherwise visitors can keep seeing the
   OLD photo for days, cached in their browser.


PUTTING IT ONLINE
-------------------
1. Make a new folder called exactly:  cst-website
2. Put ONLY these inside it:
     index.html
     images/  (with your photos in it)
   Do not include editor.html or this readme.
3. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
4. Drag the cst-website folder onto the page.
5. You get a live web address in a few seconds. Free, HTTPS included.
6. Put that address into your Google Business Profile's website field —
   that is what actually helps you get found on Google Maps and Search.


THE PRICE EDITOR (editor.html)
---------------------------------
Keep this file ONLY on your own computer. Never upload it anywhere.
Open it any time you want to change prices or add products — it writes
the correct code for you, then you paste it into index.html yourself.
See the price-editor conversation for the full how-to.


Questions, changes, or something looks broken — just ask.
