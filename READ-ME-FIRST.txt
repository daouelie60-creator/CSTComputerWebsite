CST COMPUTER — WEBSITE PROJECT
================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
----------------------
index.html      Your website. This is the only file your customers see.
editor.html     Your PRIVATE price + photo editor. Do NOT upload this one.
images/products/  The only photos the site uses: your product pictures.
                  Add them with editor.html. See below.


BEFORE YOU PUT THE SITE ONLINE
--------------------------------
1. Open index.html in Notepad or VS Code (not Word — it breaks quotes).
2. Search for CHANGE-ME. Fill in the 2 things it finds:
     - your email
     - your opening time (closing 19:30 is already set)
3. Delete the yellow "Setup" box near the top of the page
   (marked "DELETE THIS WHOLE BLOCK BEFORE YOU GO LIVE").
   Facebook: the "Follow us on Facebook" QR card and links already point
   to your page. If the page address changes, search index.html for
   facebook.com and regenerate images/facebook-qr.svg.
4. Add product photos (optional) with editor.html — drag a picture onto
   a product, copy the code into index.html, and upload the same file
   into images/products/. Products with no photo show a clean icon.
5. If you EVER replace a product photo later, open index.html, find:
     var ASSETS_V = "1";
   and change it to "2", "3", etc. Otherwise visitors can keep seeing the
   OLD photo for days, cached in their browser.


PUTTING IT ONLINE
-------------------
1. Make a new folder called exactly:  cst-website
2. Put ONLY these inside it:
     index.html
     images/  (with the images/products/ folder and any product photos)
   Do not include editor.html or this readme.
3. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
4. Drag the cst-website folder onto the page.
5. You get a live web address in a few seconds. Free, HTTPS included.
6. Put that address into your Google Business Profile's website field —
   that is what actually helps you get found on Google Maps and Search.


THE PRICE + PHOTO EDITOR (editor.html)
---------------------------------------
Keep this file ONLY on your own computer. Never upload it anywhere.
Open it any time you want to change prices, add products, or set a
product photo. Type the price in one box, drag a picture onto the
product, and it writes the correct code for you — then you paste that
into index.html yourself and upload any new photo to images/products/.


Questions, changes, or something looks broken — just ask.
