CST — PRODUCT PHOTOS
====================

This is the only folder the website uses for pictures.

To add a photo to a product:

  1. Open editor.html on your computer.
  2. On the product card, drag a photo onto the drop zone
     (or click "Choose photo").
  3. Click "Copy the code" and paste it into index.html,
     replacing the old product list.
  4. Copy the SAME image file into this folder
     (images/products/) on your host, next to index.html.

Keep each photo small — under about 400 KB — or the shop
will be slow on a phone. Square or 4:3 photos look best.

If you replace a photo but keep the same file name, open
index.html and change  var ASSETS_V = "1";  to "2", "3",
and so on, so visitors stop seeing the old picture.
